Common questions

A story got something wrong. Open the story, tap "Something's off?", and say what should change, the way you'd tell a friend. The story is rewritten and re-recorded.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Manifest Stories.

I subscribed on another phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data? Swipe a story left to delete just that one. To delete everything, open Settings in the app and tap "Clear all my data". Details in the privacy policy.
