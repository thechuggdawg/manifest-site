Terms of use

Last updated September 14, 2026

What the app is

Manifest Stories writes and voices short personal stories for relaxation and motivation. They are stories, not advice. Nothing in the app is medical, financial or legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, a therapist, a financial adviser or a lawyer.

What it will and won't write

Stories follow your wish. The app will not write sexual content, harm or revenge, anything negative about a real person, instructions (what to buy, what to take, what treatment to stop), or content about hard drugs, violence or disordered eating. If a wish has to be kept gentle, the app tells you.

Your subscription

Your first story is free to keep and replay. Making more stories, or correcting a story, needs a subscription bought through the App Store. It renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel, which you can do at any time in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple ID and Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple handles all billing and refunds.

Apple's Standard End User License Agreement applies to your use of the app.

Your content

What you tell the app stays yours. Stories written for you are yours to keep and listen to. Please don't use the app to produce content that is unlawful or harmful to others.

Availability

The app depends on outside services to write and record stories. We do our best to keep it working, but sometimes a story will fail or take longer than usual. We may change or stop features as the app develops.
