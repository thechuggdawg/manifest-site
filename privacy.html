Manifest Stories

Privacy policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

Manifest Stories writes and records short personal stories for you. To do that it needs some details about your life. This page says plainly what we keep, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

What the app stores

  • Your profile. Your first name, where you live, your age range, what you do, and the people you add (a name, who they are to you, and one note about them).
  • Your wishes and answers. What you type or dictate when you ask for a story, and the answers you give to the app's follow-up questions.
  • Your stories. The written story, its audio recording, and any corrections you send.
  • Things the app learns. Small facts pulled from your stories and answers (a place you go, something you own) so later stories fit you better. You can see and remove every one of them on the About you screen.

This is kept on our servers (hosted by Supabase in the United States) under an anonymous ID for your phone. There is no account, no email address and no password.

Where your details go

To write a story, the app sends your wish, your answers and the relevant parts of your profile to an AI writing service (Anthropic). To record it, the finished text goes to a voice service (Inworld). Both are used only to make your story. We do not use your details to train AI models, and we have no reason to believe these providers do with the data we send them; their own policies apply to what passes through them.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad tracking.
  • We never sell or share your details with anyone else.
  • The microphone is only used to turn your speech into text on your phone, when you tap the mic. Nothing you say is recorded or sent anywhere as audio.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to check whether your subscription is active and to count how often the subscription screen is shown, linked to your phone's anonymous ID. Adapty doesn't collect your advertising identifier or IP address for us. We never see your payment details.

Deleting things

  • One story: swipe it left on Your stories and tap Delete. The story, its recording and its corrections are gone for good.
  • A remembered fact or a person: remove it on the About you screen.
  • Everything: Settings, then "Clear all my data". Every story, recording, your profile and everything learned is deleted from our servers.

How long we keep things

Until you delete them. Nothing is kept for a set period and then thrown away; your stories and profile stay as long as you want them, and go when you remove them. If you delete the app without clearing your data first, the stories stay on our servers under your phone's anonymous ID, so they come back if you reinstall on the same phone. Email us if you want them removed.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Only the app, signed in with your phone's anonymous ID, can read your stories and profile. The keys for the AI writing and voice services stay on our server and are never inside the app.

Your rights

You can see everything the app holds about you inside the app (Your stories and About you), change it, and delete it, without asking us. If you would rather we do it, or you have a question about your data, email haymaker.devteam@gmail.com.

Children

The app is for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: haymaker.devteam@gmail.com.
